# Granola lecture

A lecture offered for transfer from the connected Granola account.

## Information kept

- **Title** — The title from Granola.
- **Date** — When it happened.
- **Material** — The notes and, when supplied by Granola, its transcript.

## States

### Offered

Available to transfer.

### Transferred

Already brought into the library.

### Left

The student chose to leave it in Granola.
