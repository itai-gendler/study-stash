# Study Stash

Study Stash is a student’s own lecture library, for Mac and Windows. A lecture recorded on the computer, or on an iPhone or iPad, is transcribed on that device and sent to the library with whatever the student typed while it recorded. The library writes study notes round the student’s own lines with the AI they choose, adds diagrams, plots and drawings, and files the lecture under its class. The student comes back to it on the computer, in a browser or on their phone to read, ask questions and practise, next to their Canvas work and upcoming classes, and their own AI apps can read it too.

## Intent

In the project’s words: “Record a lecture. Get notes worth studying from, with the diagrams already drawn.” Study Stash is “your own lecture library”: a lecture is “transcribed on your computer, filed under the right class and turned into study notes, next to your Canvas assignments and ready to ask about,” and “your recordings never leave your own computers.” When notes are written round what the student typed, “every line of yours is kept.”
